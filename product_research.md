# Phase 2 - Pilot Launch (Months 4-6)

## Product Launch

Launch ONLY:

### Product 1

Cold-Pressed Groundnut Oil

### Product 2

Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil

---

## Manufacturing

Use:

### Contract Manufacturing / White Labelling

Do not buy machinery.

Do not open a factory.

Do not take manufacturing risk.

---

## Initial Stock

Small batch:

- 200 to 500 bottles

Objective:

Learn.

Not scale.

---

## Quality System

Every batch should have:

- Batch Number
- Manufacturing Date
- Lab Report
- Traceability

This becomes a key differentiator.

Do NOT spend ₹30-50 lakh on machinery in Year 1.

- Packaging
- Lab Testing
- Content
- Customer Acquisition
- Community Building

> A trusted brand with customers can always build a factory later.
